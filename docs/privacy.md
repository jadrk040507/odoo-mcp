# Privacy policy

## Data use and retention

Odoo business data is processed in transit only to answer the active tool request and is not retained by this service. One tenant origin and its Odoo API key are stored; the key is encrypted with AES-256-GCM. OAuth tokens are stored as hashes. Short-lived confirmation intents retain only normalized proposed changes for up to ten minutes. Idempotency receipts are retained up to one day and contain no Odoo response payload. Minimal audit metadata is retained up to 30 days.

Cloudflare processes Worker and D1 traffic as infrastructure subprocessor; Odoo processes data under the user's own Odoo agreement. Users may disconnect to erase credential material and revoke access, and may request deletion or access through the support channel. Legal requirements may require limited preservation of security evidence. No business data is sold or used to train models by this gateway operator.
